Explore and save
Find a spot on the Explore map, open its place card and spot page, and save it. The side branch adds your own spot with Add Spot mode and ends up in the same Saved list.
1
Explore · Default
Click a pin or a row to select it.
2
Explore · Selected
Double-click the row, or press Return, to open the spot page.
3
Spot page · Sample
Click Save in the header to keep the spot.
4
Saved · List
Double-click a row to reopen its spot page.
Side branch: add your own spot
5
Explore · Add Spot mode
File > Add Spot on Map (⇧⌘N), then click the map to drop a pin.
6
Spot editor · Create
Fill in the name and details, then click Save.
7
Saved · List
Your spot lives in Saved too, tagged Added by you.
