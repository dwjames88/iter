LightBadge · Compact and Regular
LightBadge · Components/LightBadge.swift
One Light Index window: the window name always beside the number, the band word and confidence; or a hollow dashed ring and the reason when there is no forecast.
Deviation 1: no band word in compact
Compact badges (map pins, Saved, Scout and Add Stop rows) show the number and short window name but not the band word. The VoiceOver label includes it. Reproduced as built; the design pass decides on a word, a glyph or nothing.
Deviation 7: literal opacity 0.85
Low confidence fades the whole chip to 85% opacity. 0.85 is a literal in LightBadge.swift, not a token (here --opacity-low-confidence in canvas-tokens.json).
Chip width vs doc
COMPONENTS.md says min width 28 (regular) and 18 (compact). In SwiftUI the horizontal padding sits outside the min-width frame, so built chips are 36 and 26 wide at minimum. Built size is drawn.
Rule: never alone
Every score names its window. No forecast is a ring and a reason, never a number and never a low score.
