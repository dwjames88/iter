SavedRow (private) · Saved/SavedView.swift
A kept spot and today's light: category symbol, name, locality and origin on the left; a compact LightBadge with "Tomorrow" or the short no-forecast reason on the right.
Anatomy
Gap space/md, vertical padding space/xs. 32 pt category column (title3, text/secondary), name type/headline, locality type/subheadline plus ProvenanceTag (gap space/sm), trailing column gap space/xxs.
Doc vs source
COMPONENTS.md says gap space/sm between locality and tag and does not name the name-to-locality gap; source uses space/xxs between name and locality line. Source followed.
Selection and separators
The row has no custom selection; the system inset List draws the selection pill and separators. Drawn here for reference.
