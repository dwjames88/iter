Snapshots only: a labelled stand-in for a live map. Not part of the product design.
Stand-in versus placeholder
The stand-in is what the snapshot tests draw: a flat ground that projects the 45 spots at their real coordinates, with the same pin views. These artboards use a quiet map placeholder (named "Map placeholder (swap)") with the pins drawn on top at the stand-in positions; swap it for a real map image.
Real map
The product map is MapKit: standard, flat, no points of interest, with zoom stepper, compass and scale. Design it from ExploreMapPane, not from the stand-in.
