Real progress for a slow request: spinner, the stage in words, four capsules and "Step n of 4", then the elapsed time once it passes 10 seconds.
Anatomy
Centred stack, gap space/md. Capsules are 24 x 4 (space/xl by space/xs), accent up to and including the current stage, separator/default after. The place in stages 2 and 3 comes from the request ("Searching near Portland, Oregon"; "Checking the drive to <place>").
Static spinner
The system spinner is drawn as one frame.
