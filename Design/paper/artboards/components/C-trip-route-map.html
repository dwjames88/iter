TripRouteMap · Trips/TripRouteMap.swift
The route sanity check: numbered pins, the active day in the route colour, other days quieter.
Live map vs stand-in
Only the selected stop is 36 pt; other active-day pins are 28 pt in accent/emphasis; pins of other days are 28 pt map/pinInactive. The snapshot stand-in draws every active-day pin large.
Deviation: pin token
TripRouteMap.swift fills the pins of the active day with accent/emphasis; the snapshot stand-in draws map/pin. The source is drawn.
Placeholder
The map is a placeholder; Apple's cartography, compass and scale are not drawn (zoom stepper shown).
