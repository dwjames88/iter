SpotEditorSheet
SpotEditorSheet · Components/SpotEditorSheet.swift
Create a spot from a dropped pin, or edit one of your own. Anything you type is yours; a lookup never overwrites it. Fixed 520 x 750 pt.
Create and edit
Left: create (lookup filled name, place and zone, no best light ticked, button "Add Spot"). Right: edit (values held, Sunrise and Night sky ticked, button "Save").
Form scrolls
The grouped form scrolls inside the fixed 520 x 750 frame. With errors, a lookup line or a multi-line Notes (3 to 6 lines) it is taller than the frame and is cut at the divider (see the screen states). The snapshot crops it the same way.
Map and pin
The pin map is 168 high, 12 pt corners and a hairline. The mappin symbol (largest title size, accent, small shadow) has its tip on the map centre; the map moves under it. The map itself is a placeholder to swap.
