Window title bar and toolbars
Window chrome · each screen's .toolbar / .navigationTitle
Reference of the title bar and toolbar of every screen: traffic lights, sidebar toggle, title, trailing items (macOS 26 glass). Settings is its own window; see the Settings artboards.
Why this exists
The snapshots draw toolbar items as blank rounded squares and omit the traffic lights. Items here come from the SwiftUI toolbars. The sidebar has its own toolbar: New Trip (plus), tooltip "New Trip (⌘N)".
