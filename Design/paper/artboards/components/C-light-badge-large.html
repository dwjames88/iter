LightBadge · Large, Sample, No forecast
LightBadge · Components/LightBadge.swift
One Light Index window: the window name always beside the number, the band word and confidence; or a hollow dashed ring and the reason when there is no forecast.
Large badge
Headline "Sunset · 87", band word with "Likely 72–100" when the range is not a single value, then confidence. The 64 pt chip uses radius card and type/score/large.
No forecast
Compact and regular never show a reason; only the large badge prints the sentence. Short forms ("Weather off", "Offline", "Too far ahead", "Passed", "Loading") are used in rows.
