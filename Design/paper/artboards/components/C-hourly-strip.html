HourlyWeatherSection · Spot/HourlyWeatherView.swift
The day's weather hour by hour, on the timeline's x-axis; golden and blue hours tinted.
Symbols are single-colour
The app uses multicolour SF Symbols for the weather; the symbol export is one colour, drawn in text/secondary.
Hard-coded opacities
Window tints 0.2, selection 0.16, cloud 0.5, below-horizon 0.35, outlook fades 0.8 and 0.6 are literals in SpotLayout.swift (deviation 7); here they are the opacity tokens.
