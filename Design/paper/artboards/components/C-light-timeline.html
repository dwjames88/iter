LightTimelineSection, TimelineRenderer · Spot/LightTimelineView.swift
The 24 hours of light: sky by sun altitude, five windows with scores, cloud by altitude, rain chance, and a scrubber.
cloud.slash does not exist
The no-forecast note is a Label with systemImage "cloud.slash", which is missing on this macOS: the app shows the sentence with an empty icon slot. Drawn as built.
Hard-coded opacities
Window tints 0.2, selection 0.16, cloud 0.5, below-horizon 0.35, outlook fades 0.8 and 0.6 are literals in SpotLayout.swift (deviation 7); here they are the opacity tokens.
