TripContextMenu
TripContextMenu · Shell/SidebarView.swift
Acts on a trip from a sidebar row or a TripCard: Open, Duplicate, Share…, divider, Delete Trip. Undoable.
Behaviour
Duplicate creates "<name> copy" and opens it. Share… is a ShareLink to a .iter file. Delete Trip is destructive; if the trip is selected, All Trips is selected first.
