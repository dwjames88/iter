ExploreRow
ExploreRowView · Explore/ExploreListPanel.swift
One spot and its light on the chosen day: name over locality at left; badge and window start time at right.
Anatomy
Gap space/md, vertical padding space/xs inside the system List row (about 10 px each side as drawn). Name type/bodyEmphasis; locality type/caption; time type/timeSmall with monospaced digits. The badge column is a fixed 112 px slot here so columns align; the app sizes it to content and right-aligns it.
Provenance
Only "Added by you" rows carry a ProvenanceTag; Curated and Apple Maps rows do not. Scout results are the ones that carry a tag.
Hover
The row has no hover style; hovering gives the map pin a chip.
