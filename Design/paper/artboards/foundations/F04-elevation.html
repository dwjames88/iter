Elevation and materials
Materials · App/Sources (regularMaterial, bar) · tools/canvas-tokens.json
Iter draws flat: paper, cards with a hairline. Only what floats (place card, Add Spot banner, route day picker, sheets, menus) takes a system material and a soft shadow. These are canvas stand-ins for the macOS values; each sits over a coloured backdrop so the translucency shows.
Materials are system-drawn
regularMaterial and bar are Apple materials. Snapshots draw them as flat background/content; the fills here approximate the macOS 26 look and are canvas tokens, not app tokens.
Shadows are canvas only
tokens.json has no elevation tokens. The shadows come from tools/canvas-tokens.json and are CSS-only (Paper has no shadow token type). The one in-app shadow is the spot-editor pin (radius = stroke/regular).
