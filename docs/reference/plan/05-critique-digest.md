# 5 · Design critique digest

This section lists every design problem found across the flow briefs, the design audit and the system review. The 266 items below come from about 520 source findings, merged where sources reported the same problem. They are ordered by severity, then by how central they are to the core job ("when and where is the light good"). Each has its fix and the plan item that addresses it, so nothing is silently dropped. Severity is the audit's scale. Items with no audit severity show "unrated" with a proposed severity.

Counts: critical 3, major 86, minor 88, polish 36, unrated 53 (proposed: major 14, minor 29, polish 10). Where one problem is rated differently in different places, the item carries the highest rating (see K07). Anything the sources label unverified stays unverified here, notably the phone map framing.

## How to read the Phase column

The Phase column gives the plan item that fixes the problem, using the IDs in [section 4](04-phases.md) (P1.1 to P5.6). Several IDs mean the fix is split, and the first one is the main one. Phase 1 items change behaviour and copy only. Phase 2 sets the visual foundation. Phase 3 redesigns the flows. Where the row's fix says "None proposed" or "Open question", the source gave no fix, and the ID is where the decision belongs.

## Where the sources disagree

Nine places where the sources point in different directions. Each has a resolution so the plan has one answer.

### K01 · Lead recalibrations from critical to major

The audit lead lowered five first-pass ratings from critical to major, each with a reason: the scout composer button (C36, "a visible bug, but the control still works", [audit Scout loading #1](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#ai-scout--ask-typed-loading-loading-late)); broken thumbnails (C40, "looks broken but does not block the task", [audit Scout results #1](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#ai-scout--results-result-selected-toasts-offline-fallback)); the golden and blue slivers (C05, "the times are available in the card below", [audit Light timeline #1](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#light-timeline-24h-strip--cloud-panel)); the Join button (C62, "the page scrolls, so the button is reachable", [audit Join #1](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#join-shared-trip--valid-not-found-unreachable)); and the developer 404 (C84, "an edge case, but a hard dead end", [audit System states #1](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#system-states--unknown-url-and-route-loading)). The audit's final criticals are only X0, X1 and the date strip.

**Resolution.** Accept the recalibrations to major. The lead checked the screenshots and gave a reason each time. All five are fixed in Phase 1 anyway (P1.4, P1.6, P3.3).

### K02 · Capture artefacts versus real bugs

The blank phone Explore map is real ([audit X0](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#cross-cutting-findings-read-these-first)). But the phone Explore captures used a page-only CSS patch, so the West Coast pins pushed off the left edge "needs confirming on a device once X0 is fixed" ([audit Lead review (Explore)](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#3-explore-ai-scout-and-add-a-spot-explore); C03, C27). The audit also names true capture artefacts: the blank band in phone landing part 2, the cut "Waning Crescent" ([audit Sun & moon arc #7](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#sun--moon-sky-arc--facts-grid)) and the `62-styleguide-top-*` frames. The dropped pin in the Pacific "is where the capture script clicked", yet [audit Add a spot #2](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#add-a-spot--pick-mode-sheet-empty--filled-toast) and [audit Trip builder states #2](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#trip-builder--stop-selected-empty-trip-add-stop-sheet-not-found) still quote those coordinates (C45, C56, C65). Forecasts were live for the week of 2026-10-04, so the "14 of 16 pins Epic" counts are a snapshot while the cause is structural (C01). Not seen in captures: the no-forecast state, the "est." marker, the feasibility warning, the Light Index card with more than 5 windows, and arrow-key behaviour (C04, C135, C134, C95, C184).

**Resolution.** The blank phone map is a real bug. Phone map framing stays unverified until it is checked on a real phone after P1.1. The Pacific-ocean pin coordinates are a capture artefact and are not used as evidence. Only the "no mini-map / raw coordinates as place line" point stands.

### K03 · "Keep" versus "issue" for the same element

The audit praises an element in one place and flags it in another. Examples: the Light Index ring is "the best product explanation on the page" ([audit Landing desktop hero](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#landing--desktop-hero-top--full-page)) yet its score is critical ([audit X1](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#cross-cutting-findings-read-these-first); C01, C169). The score on the date tile is "the right place for it" ([audit Date strip](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#when-to-shoot--date-strip)) yet the strip is "not a reliable 'which day is best' tool" (C02). The spot page "reads top to bottom as a decision flow" ([audit Spot top](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#spot-top-full-page--default-today)) yet its first viewport does not answer "when is it good" (C07). The sheet snaps are "the right model" ([audit Explore browse](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#explore-browse--default-map--list-sheet-peekhalffull)) yet the peek shows less than one result (C29). The sky strip is "the right metaphor" yet its white mid-day band reads as empty (C10). The dotted rail is "a great idea" yet overnight legs need a day break (C50). The date and sort popovers are "Good" yet a 7-item text menu is a desktop idiom on phone (C118). The phone mock is praised ([audit Landing phone](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#landing--phone-hero-and-full-page)) yet [brief 01](../flows/01-first-visit-landing.md#not-as-it-looks) says it is hard-coded (C231). The trip-not-found copy is good yet has no code field (C204).

**Resolution.** Both sides are right: the form of each element is good, and the content or order is wrong. Keep the ring, the date tiles, the sheet model, the sky strip and the rail. Fix what they show (P1.3, P3.2, P3.3, P3.5).

### K04 · The italic gold accent and the serif voice

The audit keeps the gold italic word as a focal point and "the strongest brand moment" on Landing, Trips and Scout ([audit Landing desktop hero](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#landing--desktop-hero-top--full-page); [audit Trips list](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#trips-list--empty-templates-populated)), and 02 lists "the gold italic *light.*" among what is "genuinely Iter's" ([02 §8](../research/design-audit/02-system-and-identity.md#8-identity-distinctive-or-generic)). The same sources criticise it: serif display plus italic is "the least tech element" ([audit Landing vs brand #3](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#landing-vs-brand-direction)), and the device "is starting to feel like a template" with the advice to "drop the italic accent everywhere except one place" ([02 §2](../research/design-audit/02-system-and-identity.md#2-typography)). Items: C82, C195.

**Resolution.** Follow the system review: the italic accent survives in one place at most, as the brand moment. The serif's role is now tied to round three, where you asked for serif wordmark options. Either way, no serif or italic on data and decisions.

### K05 · Brand direction: different recommended fixes

The audit recommended Option A short term ("ship the Tittle wordmark, cut italics to one per page, make gold flat"), written against round one ([audit Landing vs brand](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#landing-vs-brand-direction)). 02 recommended "B or C. Decide with the logo, not after it" and found Meridian + Archivo the most coherent for round two ([02 §2](../research/design-audit/02-system-and-identity.md#2-typography); [02 §8](../research/design-audit/02-system-and-identity.md#fit-with-the-logo-explorations-round-1-branditerlogo-optionshtml)). Items: C82, C228.

**Resolution.** Superseded. After round two you chose the route idea (Waypoints / Switchback as favourite, Contour second) and the First Light and Alpine palettes. The audit's Tittle and Meridian recommendations are history. See [section 2.2](02-decisions.md#22-design-direction-and-identity).

### K06 · Gold: value and role

The audit asks for one flat gold, the logo's `#F5B72B` ([audit Landing vs brand #2](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#landing-vs-brand-direction)). 02 measures `#F5B72B` at 1.80:1 as a graphic on white and wants gold "in exactly one role in the UI: the brand light moment ... never as a data band", with a deepened `#BC7F00` for light grounds ([02 §3](../research/design-audit/02-system-and-identity.md#3-colour)). Yet both propose an amber ramp for score bars ("light to dark amber", [audit Light Index card #2](../research/design-audit/01-screen-by-screen-audit.md#light-index-card); "pale sand → deep amber", 02 §3). Items: C83, C13, C15.

**Resolution.** Follow the measurement: `#F5B72B` is never a graphic on white (1.80:1). A warm ramp for data is fine as long as the brand accent has a different hue or role. With your palettes, the gold moment's home is an open question ([section 2.2](02-decisions.md#22-design-direction-and-identity)).

### K07 · Same problem, different severities

The same problem is rated differently in different places. The focus ring is major as X8 but minor in Explore filters #6, Landing hero #6 and Add to trip #5 (C74). "Vantage" and developer text is major as X5 but minor in Saved #3 and Scout loading #7 (C72). Developer copy is major for the scout fallback and the router 404 but minor for "Sharing works once deployed" (C43, C84, C158). Native date inputs are part of a major in Landing hero #3 and minor in two others (C81, C109). The uniform "Epic" scale is critical in X1 but minor on the landing carousel and the Trips card dot (C01, C169, C148, C65). `#B0B0B0` informational text is major for tab labels and the hour axis, minor for scout steps, "est." and Join "Day N" (C70, C09, C125, C135, C156).

**Resolution.** A merged item carries the highest severity any source gives it. The lower ratings were local views of the same problem.

### K08 · Proposed fixes that point in different directions

- **Join name (C60, C63).** Audit Join #2 offers a required name or a "Guest" default; Top 5 Trips #1 says "optional or defaulted"; Invite #1 says require a name before copying; [brief 09](../flows/09-join-shared-trip.md#not-as-it-looks) notes Enter already bypasses the disabled state and joins as "You".
- **Hero search (C81).** The audit proposes a styled date button; [brief 01](../flows/01-first-visit-landing.md#tweak-points) proposes reading `q`, `date`, `light` in Explore or removing the card for a single "Open map" CTA.
- **"Plan a trip" (C67, C68).** The audit says open the New trip modal or rename it; [brief 06](../flows/06-trips-list-and-create.md#tweak-points) leaves it open.
- **Light score (C01, C02).** Option A relabels the ring "Best window · 87", option B is per-intent, and 02 §5.1 recommends C (intent sets the default, the name is always visible).
- **Day colours (C52).** Option A reduces to 5-6 days; option B uses black pins with only the selected day's route coloured; 02 §3 says no more than 6 hues, but trips run 1 to 21 days.
- **Add stop (C56).** The audit wants a default sort by distance from the previous stop; brief 07 records only the missing region filter and light sort.
- **Reorder (C49).** The audit and 02 propose drag handles; brief 07 leaves open whether arrows give way to drag.

**Resolution.** Join name: the joiner types a name. No "Guest" default (it would recreate the "You" leak), and the Enter bypass is fixed. Hero search: wire it to Explore (P3.8). "Plan a trip": one primary action, settled with the navigation model (P3.1, [section 6.1](06-design-options.md#61-navigation-model)). Light score: intent plus the window name ([decision 2.3](02-decisions.md#23-what-the-light-index-is-and-how-it-earns-trust)). Day colours: at most 6 categorical hues plus the day number, with only the selected day's route in colour (audit option B combined with the system review). Add stop: sort by distance from the previous stop and by light, both. Reorder: drag with undo (P3.5).

### K09 · Flow brief and audit describe the same control differently

The flow briefs describe behaviour from the code; the audit describes what the screenshots show. Stop delete: the audit calls the up/down/trash cluster "the loudest control on every card"; brief 07 says the trash sits at "60% opacity until hover from `md`" (C49). Session chips: the audit explains "Sunrise" selected beside a "Blue hour" readout as the code picking the best window inside the session; brief 07 says Explore-added stops carry a raw `bestLight` such as "midday" that maps to no chip. Both support C47. Warnings: the audit asks for a loud banner; brief 07 says warnings "will effectively never fire" across days (C134, C136). Route loading: the audit proposes adopting `RouteFallback`; brief 11 says the real router does not use it (C172). Scout toasts: the audit praises the toast; brief 03 says it names only the trip while silently creating one (C128, C241). Rename counts differ in scope, not in fact: 27 UI strings versus 9 places for "Ask Vantage" (C72). Tonight's light: the audit calls the scores "Epic or Great"; brief 01 adds that on a forecast failure they silently show a flat 58 (C169, C04).

**Resolution.** For behaviour, the flow brief's code-level facts win. For what users perceive, the audit wins. They do not contradict; both feed the same fix.
