# 7 · Decisions needed from you, and what we still do not know

## Decisions, in the order they block work

Each is short to answer. The first four unblock Phases 1 and 2.

1. **Positioning: light-first road trips for the serious hobbyist?** Frames every other choice, from the home screen to what gets cut. Recommended: yes ([2.1](02-decisions.md#21-positioning-and-first-audience)).
2. **Is "Iter" the name in all UI copy now?** "Vantage" appears 27 times in UI copy and is baked into the Logo component and the "Ask Vantage" label in Figma. Blocks P1.4 and the screen captures ([tool path §4](../research/design-audit/04-design-tool-path.md#4-what-i-would-prepare-so-the-import-takes-one-sitting)).
3. **The Light Index rule.** Intent-based score with the window name always shown, plus a real "No forecast" state. Blocks P1.3 and P3.4 ([2.3](02-decisions.md#23-what-the-light-index-is-and-how-it-earns-trust)).
4. **How to get the screens into Figma.** The no-quota route (development plugin for components, html.to.design for screens), or one month of Figma Professional ($16/mo as listed) to do it all in a day. Blocks P2.2 ([3.2](03-design-control.md#32-getting-everything-into-figma)).
5. **Identity: the mark from round three, and how First Light and Alpine share the work.** Your direction is set (route idea; First Light and Alpine); the open choices are which palette leads, where the light moment lives, and the type. Lean: Alpine for the interface and route, coral for the light. Blocks P2.4 to P2.6 and everything visual after them ([2.2](02-decisions.md#22-design-direction-and-identity), [6.5](06-design-options.md#65-visual-identity-direction)).
6. **Navigation model.** Recommended: trip-first, with Explore one tap away and Now appearing during a trip. Blocks P3.1 ([6.1](06-design-options.md#61-navigation-model)).
7. **Map and sheet, trip builder, and Light Index presentation.** Three design choices, each with a lean. They block P3.2, P3.4 and P3.5 ([6.2](06-design-options.md#62-map-and-sheet-behaviour), [6.3](06-design-options.md#63-trip-builder-interaction), [6.4](06-design-options.md#64-light-index-presentation)).
8. **Scope of the first native release.** Recommended: plan and field (A1, B2, B1 as a suggestion, C2, C1). Blocks Phase 5 ([2.4](02-decisions.md#24-scope-of-the-first-native-release)).
9. **Data providers.** Recommended: the Apple-native stack. Blocks any public or paid launch, not design ([2.6](02-decisions.md#26-data-providers-licensed-for-commercial-use)).
10. **Business model.** Recommended: free core, one paid tier, offline never paywalled. Blocks the paywall design (P5.6) ([2.5](02-decisions.md#25-business-model-in-outline)).

The research is wide but has known holes. None of them stops Phases 1 to 3. Several of them matter before Phase 4's bets are built, and each can be closed cheaply.
