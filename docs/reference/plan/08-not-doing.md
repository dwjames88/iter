# 8 · What we are deliberately not doing

Saying no is part of the plan. Each item below was considered in the research and set aside, or is held back on purpose. Any of them can come back if the evidence changes.

| Not doing | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Calculators: exposure, depth of field, time-lapse** | PhotoPills owns them, and people keep it for those jobs. Iter says so in onboarding instead of competing | [Positioning, Direction 1](../research/opportunities/04-positioning.md#direction-1--light-first-road-trips-recommended) |
| **AR sun path (C8)** | Table stakes that PhotoPills, TPE, Sun Surveyor and PeakFinder already have; relies on a compass that gloves disturb; geotracking coverage of remote parks is doubtful (Inferred). Map sun lines and Look Around (B6) cover the need | [Set aside](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#options-we-considered-and-set-aside) |
| **A CarPlay app** | No listed category fits a trip planner, and eligibility is unconfirmed. The Live Activity reaches the CarPlay Dashboard from any app | [Set aside](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#options-we-considered-and-set-aside) |
| **A drone-rules engine** | A wrong "green" is a legal and safety harm, and drone data licensing is awkward (OpenAIP is CC BY-NC). Link out to NPS policy and an FAA supplier instead | [Set aside](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#options-we-considered-and-set-aside); [audience 5.3](../research/opportunities/01-photographers-and-jobs.md#53-who-not-to-design-for-yet-and-why) |
| **A social feed or public spot leaderboard** | Invites the crowd damage the research documents, needs moderation, and conflicts with private-by-default (D1) | [Set aside](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#options-we-considered-and-set-aside) |
| **Creator content calendars** | Thin evidence; creators plan by video list, not light; low willingness to pay | [Set aside](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#options-we-considered-and-set-aside) |
| **A general travel planner** (hotels, restaurants, flights) | Outside the promise, and it dilutes the first story | [Positioning, Direction 1](../research/opportunities/04-positioning.md#direction-1--light-first-road-trips-recommended) |
| **Designing for pros, tour leaders, drone pilots, van-lifers or creators first** | Well served elsewhere, a legal minefield, or no evidence to design from | [Audience 5.3](../research/opportunities/01-photographers-and-jobs.md#53-who-not-to-design-for-yet-and-why) |
| **Deep astro tooling** beyond a night score | The second audience, not the first; it needs data Iter lacks (Bortle, Milky Way core, humidity), and the Falchi atlas cannot ship in a paid app | [Audience 5.2](../research/opportunities/01-photographers-and-jobs.md#52-the-second-audience); [where the prototype stands](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#where-the-prototype-stands) |
| **Competing on forecast accuracy** | The most crowded, cheapest lane, bounded by weather models. Iter wins on explanation and honesty, not on skill | [Direction 3](../research/opportunities/04-positioning.md#direction-3--the-honest-sky-forecaster) |
| **AI as the headline** | Everyone is adding it, and Google's will be free. Iter's edge is where the result lands: an editable, light-aware trip | [Gaps §6, opening 8](../research/competitors/gaps-and-openings.md#8-ai-that-plans-a-trip-and-shows-its-work) |
| **Accounts before value** | No-account sharing is a strength. Identity comes later, at a moment of value (iCloud on native) | [Benchmarks #24](../research/design-audit/03-benchmarks.md#24-guests-never-hit-an-account-wall), [#25](../research/design-audit/03-benchmarks.md#25-identity-later-at-a-moment-of-value) |
| **Stop caps, trip caps, or paywalled offline** | The shape the category punishes in reviews | [Gaps §4](../research/competitors/gaps-and-openings.md#4-the-ux-failures-common-across-the-category); [E5](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#e5--honest-pricing) |
| **Terrain (A4), Photos-library memory (C3), seasonal layers (A6), tides (A7), crowd timing (B5), printed trip books** in the first native release | Each is real but later: A4 and C3 come right after the first release; the rest wait for demand or data | [Strongest eight](../research/opportunities/03-differentiation-options.md#strongest-eight); [gaps §2](../research/competitors/gaps-and-openings.md#2-features-only-one-or-two-competitors-have) |
| **Redesigning screens before the foundation exists** | Every screen would be redesigned twice: once now, once after the identity and light ramp are set | This plan, [section 4](04-phases.md#why-this-order) |
| **Moving the design to Paper** | The library lives in Figma, and Paper has no documented export back. Paper stays a sketchpad at most | [Tool path §3](../research/design-audit/04-design-tool-path.md#3-paper-paperdesign) |
| **Implementing any of this now** | This is a proposal for you to react to and reshape. Nothing in it is being built yet | — |
